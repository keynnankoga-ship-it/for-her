A letter from my heart

There are things a normal text message simply cannot hold.

“Some people are beautiful because of how they look. Others become unforgettable because of how they make the world feel.”
My dearest Jaber,

I wanted to do something more intentional than sending a few lines in a chat. So I built this page, not because code can perfectly explain how I feel, but because I wanted to give those feelings a home. Every section here represents a thought I have carried quietly: that there is something about you worth choosing, protecting, nurturing and taking seriously.

I admire the person you are. I admire your presence, your mind, your softness, your strength, and the little details that make you unmistakably yourself. I do not want to rush you or turn affection into pressure. What I want is the chance to know you deeply, love you honestly, support your dreams, stand beside you through difficult seasons, celebrate your victories, and build memories that become the kind of stories we will still smile about years from now.

When I say I see you as someone I could one day call my wife, I do not mean I am trying to skip the beautiful stages in between. I mean that my intentions are not casual. I want the friendship, the courtship, the dates, the awkward moments, the growing together, the disagreements we learn to handle with maturity, the spontaneous adventures, the quiet Sundays, and the ordinary days that slowly become an extraordinary life.

I cannot promise perfection. I can promise intention. I can promise honesty. I can promise effort. I can promise that if you give me the chance to be your man, I will treat that chance as something precious, not something I am entitled to.

You deserve to be chosen deliberately, loved gently, respected completely, and reminded often that your presence matters. That is the kind of relationship I want us to build, if your heart is willing to explore it with mine.

— Koyo ❤
